# Independent NVIDIA T4 overlap runtime audit

## Purpose

Quantify the relative runtime cost of the three crop geometries using the same
DeepLabV3--MobileNetV3-Large architecture and the original
`predict_partition` execution path.

## Protocol

- Platform: NVIDIA T4.
- Framework: PyTorch 1.13.0 + CUDA 11.6 runtime.
- Runs: 500 after 50 warm-up iterations per geometry.
- Statistic: mean, p99, p99.5, and maximum wall-clock time.
- Included path: crop preparation, GPU forward passes, sigmoid, GPU-to-host
  probability transfer, and NumPy accumulation/fusion.
- Random inputs and randomly initialized weights are used because execution
  timing does not depend on the learned parameter values; no perception metric
  is generated by this audit.

## Results

| Geometry | Mean (ms) | p99 (ms) | p99.5 (ms) | Mean ratio | p99.5 ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Non-overlap, 4 crops | 54.768 | 60.839 | 63.349 | 1.000 | 1.000 |
| Mild overlap, 4 crops | 55.158 | 62.460 | 64.136 | 1.007 | 1.012 |
| Heavy overlap, 5 crops | 67.362 | 75.936 | 81.391 | 1.230 | 1.285 |

## Interpretation boundary

These are independently measured prototype NVIDIA T4 values. They support a
paired relative-cost comparison among crop geometries. They are kept distinct
from the frozen per-path T4 scheduling budgets because the audit measures a
different pipeline: crop preparation, sequential forward passes, transfer, and
fusion.

Raw machine-readable output: `overlap_runtime_local.json`.
